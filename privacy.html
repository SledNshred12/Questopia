Questopia

Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026

The short version

  • Questopia is for people 13 and older.
  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your own device.
  • If you sign in with Google, we save your progress online so it follows you to other devices.
  • Friends you accept can see your name, avatar, title, unlocked items and points. Nobody can see your email.
  • We don't sell your information or show ads. Your phone's location is used only while you run an Activity, and it stays on your device. We never see it. The optional Street and Satellite map views load map pictures from OpenStreetMap or Esri (see below).
  • You can ask us to delete everything at questopiagame@gmail.com.

What Questopia is

Questopia is a game that gets you outside. You do quests, earn points, and spend them on looks for your avatar. Quests need proof. Walk and hike quests use your phone's GPS through an Activity, to check that you really went for a walk. Other quests ask you to take a photo with your camera, and the game checks it on your phone. Scavenger Hunts let you share photos with your friends (see below). Apart from that, the game never asks for your camera or your photos.

We don't collect your real name (unless you used it as your username or it's in your Google name), your address, your phone number, your contacts, or any payment information. We don't collect your location either: during an Activity it is used on your phone only (see below). Questopia has no purchases.

Who can see what

  • Friends you've accepted can see your username, avatar, title, the items you've unlocked and how many points you have.
  • A person who adds your friend code sends you a request. You see their username, avatar and title. They see your username after sending it. They don't see more unless you accept.
  • Scavenger Hunt photos you post can be seen by your accepted friends. Photos you send back can be seen by the person whose hunt it is.
  • Nobody else can see where you walked. Routes and walk history stay on your device only.
  • Map views (optional). The route map starts in Simple view, which loads nothing from the internet. If you choose Street or Satellite, your phone downloads small map pictures from OpenStreetMap or Esri. Those services can see which area of the map is on screen and your phone's IP address. They never get your route line, your account or your name. Your choice is remembered on your device.
  • No other player can see your email address, and people who aren't your accepted friends can't see your points. Your friend code only lets people send you a request.
  • The person who runs Questopia can see the saved data in the database, such as emails and game progress, in order to run and fix the game. That information is not shared or sold.

Where it's kept, and who helps us run it

  • On your device: the game saves your progress in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Online (only if you sign in): Supabase stores accounts and saved games.
  • Google handles the sign-in. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and delivers the game's 3D code. Like any website host, it sees technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the page. The game also loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

These companies only handle information to provide those services. We don't send your data to anyone else, and we don't use your Google information for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your saved game until you ask us to delete it. If you stop using Questopia, you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

  • Play without an account. Don't sign in and nothing is saved online.
  • Sign out. Your progress stays on your device.
  • Remove a friend or decline a request in the Friends screen.
  • Delete everything. Email questopiagame@gmail.com from the email you used to sign in, and we'll delete your account, saved game and friend connections. You can also remove Questopia's access in your Google account settings.

Age

Questopia is for people 13 and older. If you're under 13, please don't create an account. If you think someone under 13 has signed in, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes and contact

If we change how Questopia handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions, or want your data deleted? Email questopiagame@gmail.com.
